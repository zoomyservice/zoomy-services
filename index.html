Phone agents

It picks up when you can’t.

Busy day, late evening, day off. Your phone agent knows your business and your hours, takes the order and reads it back. The moment the call ends, your team gets it by text message and email.

  • English, Spanish and more. It switches when the caller does.
  • Knows what day it is. “Tomorrow” always lands on the right date.
  • Never asks for card numbers. Payment stays with you.

Good questions

Something else on your mind? Ask the assistant in the corner, or email us.

How much does it cost?

Every business needs something a little different, so we quote each project. You get a fixed price in writing before any work starts.

Can I really update it myself?

Yes. Products, prices, photos, hours and announcements, from your phone or computer. If you can send a text, you can use the panel.

Who owns the website?

You do. We can host it for you, or connect it to the domain you already have.

Do I need a new phone number?

No. The phone agent can have its own number, or your current line can forward to it when you’re busy or closed.
